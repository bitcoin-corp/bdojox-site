一

What bDojoX is

A training hall for company registers.

A dojo is where you practise until the basics are second nature. bDojoX does that for the paperwork every company has to keep: who holds which shares.

ドージョー
01

For any company

Three friends with a shop or a firm with hundreds of members. The company keeps its own register; bShares is the software it keeps it with.

02

Held by your shareholders

Each holder sees their shares in their own bWalletX, in a 2-of-2 lock: the holder's key and the company's, so nothing moves unless both agree.

03

Form it, then raise

Starting out? bDojoX is built to help a new company form on chain and, in time, raise capital in BSV and MNEE from stakeholders' wallets, with the care securities need.

二

How it works for a company

Five belts, five stages of a company.

Each belt is a real milestone, from your first token to a public listing. The register of members stays the legal record at every step.

オビ
  1. 01

    White belt · Token launched

    Your company's token is live on chain, with its own room on bChatX for the people who hold it.

    Live
  2. 02

    Yellow belt · Partnership shares, locked

    Partners' shares sit in 2-of-2 multisig in each partner's bWalletX, so nothing moves unless both sides sign.

    Live
  3. 03

    Green belt · Registered company

    Your register of members is kept in bShares and mirrored on chain as tokens, entry for entry. If the two ever differ, the register wins.

    Testing
  4. 04

    Blue belt · Capital raised

    You raise in BSV and MNEE from verified investors, each allotment recorded on the register and held in the investor's own wallet.

    Coming
  5. 05

    Black belt · Public listing

    Your shares are offered to the public through the proper regulated route. $403, our securities protocol on verified identity, is being built for this. Nothing on this page is an offer of shares.

    Planned
