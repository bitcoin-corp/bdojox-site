一

What bDojoX is

A training hall for company registers.

A dojo is where you practise until the basics are second nature. bDojoX does that for the paperwork every company has to keep: who holds which shares.

ドージョー
01

For any company

Three friends with a shop or a firm with hundreds of members. The company keeps its own register; bShares is the software it keeps it with.

02

Held by your shareholders

Each holder sees their shares in their own bWalletX, in a 2-of-2 lock: the holder's key and the company's, so nothing moves unless both agree.

03

Form it, then raise

Starting out? bDojoX is built to help a new company form on chain and, in time, raise capital in BSV from stakeholders' wallets, with the care securities need.

二

How it works for a company

Five belts, one register.

You move up one step at a time. The register of members stays the legal record at every step; everything else follows it.

オビ
  1. 01

    Keep the register

    Enter your members and holdings in bShares. Your directors approve every entry, as company law asks.

    Testing
  2. 02

    Mirror it on chain

    A tokenised record follows the register entry for entry. If the two ever differ, the register wins.

    Testing
  3. 03

    Into shareholders' wallets

    Each member sees their holding in their own bWalletX, next to everything else they own.

    Coming
  4. 04

    Locked 2-of-2

    Multisig locks already work in bWalletX. For shares, a transfer needs the holder and the company to sign, so records only move when the directors approve.

    Live
  5. 05

    $403, later

    $403 is our planned protocol for securities-grade access, built on verified identity. Nothing on this page is an offer of shares, and nothing will be offered to the public through it.

    Planned
