# Style guide

- **Palette:** deep navy #07090f (shadow lift), red #e0262f, blue #3a6bf0, white. Red light leak bottom-left, blue top-right, both breathing slowly.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display Black (900) for the beat words, in CAPITALS with a red offset shadow that settles on the slam; IBM Plex Sans Condensed SemiBold for labels ("01 · FORM YOUR COMPANY ON CHAIN") and the HUD. Ghost kanji (Hiragino W8, 10% white) behind each beat: 発進 形 資金 株.
- **Layout:** desktop words bottom-left third (clears the site's headline gradient on the left, still reads on /watch); mobile words centred at 62% height, kanji top.
- **Motion:** Ken Burns push or pull on every shot (ease-in-out mixed with linear), camera shake on downbeat hits, diagonal sword-slash wipes with a hot white-red edge, whip-pans with motion blur, white flash on big hits, lightning in the open, a light glint along the katana, two-layer parallax rain, spark bursts on the clash and the slash, falling petals in the breath, a hanko seal (torii mark, ink texture) stamped onto the register on a beat.
- **Finish:** contrast 1.12, navy lift, vignette, a small exposure pulse on every beat (120 BPM), 6-frame film grain loop, HUD corners with REC timecode and a LAUNCH / FORM / RAISE / SHARES progress row.
- **Loop:** opens from black, ends on black (last 0.5 s), so the muted hero loop is seamless.
